# International Women's Soccer: A Data Story (1991–2026)

[![Tableau Public](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tableau-Public-blue?logo=tableau)](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/kristin.mooney/viz/WomensSoccerAnalysis/InternationalWomensSoccerADataStory19912026)

An interactive three-part Tableau story exploring 35 years of international women's soccer — from a 25-nation tournament in 1991 to a global sport with 200+ competing nations in 2025. This project analyzes growth, competitiveness, scoring trends, home field advantage, and team KPI performance across 6,966 matches.

## Dashboard Overview

### Dashboard 1 — 35 Years of Women's Soccer: A Global Overview
![Dashboard 1 - Global Overview](<International Soccer Growth.png>)

A high-level look at the explosive growth of women's international soccer since the first Women's World Cup in 1991. Includes:
- **Nations Playing Each Year** — line chart showing growth from 25 (1991) to 201 (2025), with a visible COVID-19 dip
- **Matches Have Gotten More Competitive Over Time** — combined chart showing how average goal differential has dropped from 3.95 to 2.25
- **Win Dominance by Home Field Advantage** — scatter plot highlighting USA, Germany, France, and North Korea
- **Global Reach Map** — world map shaded by home match hosting activity

---

### Dashboard 2 — Modern Era: Scoring Trends & Home Field Advantage (2021–2026)
![Dashboard 2 - Modern Era](<Scoreline Analysis.png>)

A deep dive into scoring patterns and match dynamics in the modern era. Includes:
- **Average Goals Per Match Trending** — dipped from 1.97 (2021) to 1.59 (2024), now recovering to 1.78 (2026)
- **Top 15 Teams by Goals Scored** — Spain leads (298), followed by England (265) and USA (263)
- **Top 10 Most Common Scorelines** — 1-0 is the most common result, reinforcing growing competitiveness
- **Home Field Advantage** — home teams average 2.07 goals vs. 1.36 away (55% more), except England and Italy who perform better on the road

---

### Dashboard 3 — Team Deep Dive: KPI Performance Dashboard (Top 20 Teams, 1991–2026)
![Dashboard 3 - KPI Dashboard](<KPI Dashboard.png>)

An interactive team-level performance explorer. Includes:
- **KPI Tiles** — 6,966 total matches | 56% overall win rate | 2.33 avg goals scored | 2.47 avg goal differential
- **Match Outcome Breakdown** — win/loss/draw proportions for all 20 teams
- **Win Rate by Team** — USA leads at 80%, followed by Germany (72%) and France (66%)
- **Goals Scored vs. Conceded** — USA scores 3.03 goals/match and concedes just 0.55

> Use the **Team** dropdown to filter all KPIs and charts to a single nation.

## Key Findings

| Insight | Detail |
|---|---|
| Massive Growth | Women's soccer grew from 25 nations (1991) to 201 nations (2025) |
| More Competitive | Avg goal differential dropped from 3.95 → 2.25 over 35 years |
| USA Dominance | 80% win rate, but avg goal differential dropped from 6.27 → 2.60 |
| Home Field Matters | Home teams score 55% more goals on average than away teams |
| Tight Games | The most common scoreline is 1-0, followed by 2-0 and 2-1 |
| New Scoring Powers | Spain, Japan & Germany driving the recent uptick in goals per match |
